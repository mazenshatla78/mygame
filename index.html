HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Goal

Find all 8 diary pages scattered around the school, then escape through the front doors. Find the 5 hidden tapes for the secret ending.

🧹 Mr. Mop

The janitor patrols the halls. If he sees you — run, hide in a locker, or throw a hall pass to distract him. Running is loud. Your flashlight is bright. Both attract him.

⌨️ Controls

WASD / Arrows move · Shift run · E interact / open / hide · F flashlight · Q use soda · R throw hall pass · Esc pause

📱 Mobile

Left thumb-stick to move. Buttons to interact, run, and use your flashlight.

🔦 Survival

Your flashlight battery drains. Find batteries. Soda restores stamina. Every page you take makes Mr. Mop faster and angrier.

👂 Listen

He hums when he's near. Your heartbeat gets louder when he's close. Trust your ears.
